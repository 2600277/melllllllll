Why I love you

I don’t even know how to explain how much I love you without it sounding like I’m just saying words, but I really do love you with everything in me.

It’s crazy to think that we met two years ago in November, because back then I never would’ve imagined you’d become such a huge part of my life and my heart. So much has happened between then and now, but somehow I still find myself loving you just as much, if not more.

You’ve seen different sides of me, even the parts I don’t always show people, and I’m grateful that you still chose to stay and love me. I know I’m not perfect and I know I don’t always say or do things the right way, but my love for you has always been real.

I care about you so much, and I honestly don’t want to imagine my life without you in it. I’m forever grateful for that day in November when we met, because it gave me the chance to know you, love you, and have someone as special as you in my life.

I love you more than I’ll ever be able to put into words, and I hope you always remember that.
